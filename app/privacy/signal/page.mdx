# Signal Privacy Policy

<p className="updated">Effective date: August 13, 2026</p>

grittyworld ("we", "us") operates the game **Signal** (the "Game"). This policy
explains what information the Game handles, why, and what choices you have.

## Information we collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game progress | Save files, chapter progress, settings | To let you resume where you left off |
| Device and diagnostic data | Game version, OS version, device model, crash logs | To diagnose crashes and improve stability |

The Game does not require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email
address, contacts, location, or photos, and we do not sell personal information.

## How we use information

- To save and restore your progress
- To diagnose errors and improve the Game's stability
- To respond to your support requests

## Storage and retention

Save data and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Game
removes them. Diagnostic data is retained only as long as needed to investigate
stability issues.

## Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties for advertising. Data may be
processed by infrastructure providers acting on our behalf solely to operate the
Game, and we may disclose information where required by law.

## Your rights

Because the Game stores data locally and without an account, you can remove all
associated data by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. For any other
request, email us at the address below.

## Children

The Game is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the Game changes. Material changes will be
reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page.
