# 발품 (balpoom) Privacy Policy

<p className="updated">Effective date: August 13, 2026</p>

grittyworld ("we", "us") operates the mobile application **발품 (balpoom)** (the
"App"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and what
choices you have.

## Information we collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content you create | Property records, notes, photos, ratings, folders | To provide the core function of the App — saving and organizing your visit records |
| Location data | Address or coordinates you attach to a record | To place your records on a map and let you sort by distance. Collected only when you grant location permission |
| Photos | Images you choose from your library or capture in the App | To attach visual records to a property entry. We access only the images you explicitly select |
| Device and diagnostic data | App version, OS version, device model, crash logs | To diagnose crashes and improve stability |

We do **not** collect your name, phone number, or precise background location,
and we do not sell personal information.

## How we use information

- To store and display the records you create
- To sync your data across your own devices, if you enable sync
- To diagnose errors and improve the App's reliability
- To respond to your support requests

## Storage and retention

Records you create are stored on your device. If you enable account-based sync,
they are also stored on our hosting provider's servers so they can be restored
on your devices. We retain synced data until you delete the record or your
account, after which it is removed from active systems within 30 days.

## Sharing

We do not share your content with third parties for advertising. Data may be
processed by infrastructure providers acting on our behalf solely to operate the
App, and we may disclose information where required by law.

## Your rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your records at any time from within
the App. To request deletion of all account data, email us at the address below.
You may also revoke location or photo permissions at any time in your device
settings; the App will continue to work with those features disabled.

## Children

The App is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be reflected
by a new effective date at the top of this page.
