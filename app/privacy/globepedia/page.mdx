# Globepedia Privacy Policy

<p className="updated">Effective date: August 13, 2026</p>

grittyworld ("we", "us") operates the mobile application **Globepedia** (the
"App"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and what
choices you have.

## Information we collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App preferences | Favorites, visited markers, language and display settings | To remember your progress and preferences between sessions |
| Device and diagnostic data | App version, OS version, device model, crash logs | To diagnose crashes and improve stability |

The App does not require an account. We do **not** collect your name, email
address, contacts, precise location, or photos, and we do not sell personal
information.

## How we use information

- To remember your settings and the countries you have explored
- To diagnose errors and improve the App's reliability
- To respond to your support requests

## Storage and retention

Your preferences and progress are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling
the App removes them. Diagnostic data is retained only as long as needed to
investigate stability issues.

## Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties for advertising. Data may be
processed by infrastructure providers acting on our behalf solely to operate the
App, and we may disclose information where required by law.

## Your rights

Because the App stores data locally and without an account, you can remove all
associated data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. For any other
request, email us at the address below.

## Children

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 14.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be reflected
by a new effective date at the top of this page.
